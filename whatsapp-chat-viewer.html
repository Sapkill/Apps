WhatsApp Chat Viewer

Replay an exported chat the way it looked on the phone, from either side of the conversation, jump to any date and dig up the fun stats.

How do I export a chat? ▾
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp and tap the contact or group name.
  2. Choose Export chat → Without media.
  3. iPhone gives a .zip: unzip it and use the _chat.txt inside. Android gives the .txt directly.
Chats are read inside this browser and never uploaded anywhere.
Read-only replay · drop another .txt anywhere to switch chats
